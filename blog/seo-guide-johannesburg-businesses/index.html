Local SEO Guide for Johannesburg Businesses

A beginner-friendly guide to showing up when nearby customers search for what you do.

7 min read · Updated August 2026

Local SEO is how your business shows up when someone nearby searches for "plumber near me" or "web design Johannesburg" - both in regular Google search results and in the map pack that appears above them. For a business that depends on local customers, it's usually the highest-return marketing you can do, and most of it doesn't cost anything but time.

Start with your Google Business Profile

This is the single most important local SEO asset you have, and it's free. Claim it, verify it, and fill in every field properly: correct business name, category, service area, hours, phone number, and a real description of what you do. Add photos regularly - businesses with more photos consistently get more clicks and calls.

Consistency matters: your business name, address, and phone number should be written exactly the same way everywhere online - your website, your Google profile, directory listings. Inconsistent details (e.g. "St" vs "Street") quietly undermine how much Google trusts your business.

Get your on-page basics right

Every page on your website should have a clear title tag and meta description that mentions what you do and where you do it - not stuffed with keywords, just clear and specific. A page titled "Home" tells Google nothing; a page titled "Plumber in Sandton | 24-Hour Emergency Callouts" tells it exactly what you offer and where.

Build location-specific content honestly

If you serve multiple suburbs or towns, it's tempting to create one page per area by copying the same text and swapping the place name. Google's algorithms - and real visitors - can tell when that's happened, and it can actively hurt your rankings across all of those pages. Each area page needs a genuine reason to exist: real local detail, not just a different heading.

Reviews are a ranking factor, not just social proof

The number, recency, and quality of your Google reviews directly influences local rankings, on top of the obvious trust benefit. Ask happy customers directly and make it easy - a text with a direct link works better than hoping they'll find your profile themselves.

Get listed consistently across directories

Beyond Google, make sure your business is listed accurately on the directories relevant to South African businesses. These citations reinforce to Google that your business is real, established, and located where you say it is.

What to expect, realistically

Local SEO is not instant. Meaningful movement usually takes a few months of consistent work, not a few days. But unlike paid ads, the work compounds - a well-optimised profile and a properly built set of pages keep working for you long after you've stopped actively touching them.
