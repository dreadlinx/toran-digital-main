How Much Does a Website Cost in Johannesburg?

A straight answer, with real numbers, so you can budget before you start asking for quotes.

6 min read · Updated August 2026

The honest answer is: it depends what the website needs to do. But "it depends" is not much use when you're trying to budget, so here's a real breakdown of what different types of business websites cost in Johannesburg right now, and what actually changes the price.

Starter business website: from R3,500

This covers a clean, mobile-friendly site for a business that mainly needs to explain what it does, show proof of work, and make it easy to get in touch - a handful of pages, a working contact form, and basic on-page SEO so the site can actually be found. This is the right starting point for most trades, consultants, and small service businesses.

Corporate Pro website: from R6,500

This tier is for businesses that need more structure - more service pages, more content, tighter design, and a build that's set up to grow into things like a blog or a bigger service catalogue later. It suits businesses with a few different offerings that each deserve their own page.

E-commerce website: from R9,500

Online stores cost more because there's genuinely more to build: product management, secure payment integration, inventory handling, and a checkout flow that doesn't lose sales to friction. Final pricing here depends heavily on your product catalogue size and any specific integrations you need.

Be wary of quotes that are suspiciously low (e.g. under R1,500). Often, these use pirated themes, lack essential security updates, and can leave you owning nothing you can actually transfer to another host or developer later.

What actually moves the price

  • Number of pages and content depth: more service/location pages means more writing and structure.
  • Custom design vs. template: a bespoke layout takes longer than adapting a proven one.
  • Functionality: booking systems, calculators, client portals, and integrations all add real build time.
  • Content and photography: if you need copywriting or professional photos, that's separate from the build itself.
  • Ongoing hosting and maintenance: ask whether this is included for a period, and what it costs after that.

What a fair quote should tell you upfront

A quote worth trusting should be specific about page count, who owns the domain and files after launch, how the site works on mobile, what's included in the price versus billed separately, and what support looks like once the site is live. If a quote is vague on any of these, ask before you sign anything.
