Capture Creative vs Toran Digital: Which Benoni Design Partner Fits Your Project?

6 min read · Updated August 2026

Choosing a design or digital partner is about fit, not finding one company that is right for every job. Capture Creative and Toran Digital both serve Benoni and the East Rand, but their public service mix is different.

This comparison is based on the services each business describes on its public website. It is not a rating, and it does not claim that one business is better than the other. The useful question is which one matches the work you need now.

When Capture Creative may be a good fit

Capture Creative may suit a business looking mainly for graphic design, corporate identity, print advertising, website design, ecommerce, or industry-specific creative work. Its public site also shows a broad set of sector pages across the East Rand.

When Toran Digital may be a good fit

Toran Digital may suit a business that wants to discuss website work alongside mobile apps, custom web tools, local search, vehicle branding, or a wider digital project. The best fit depends on the actual scope, proof, communication style, and delivery plan offered for your project.

What to compare before you decide

  1. Ask to see relevant examples, not only general portfolio work.
  2. Ask who will manage the project and how feedback is handled.
  3. Confirm what is included, what is not included, and what happens after launch.
  4. Compare the project plan, not only the first price.
  5. Make sure you understand ownership of design files, website access, domains, and accounts.
Fair comparison note: services change over time. Check each company’s current website and speak to both teams before deciding.
