## Toran Digital Frontend Aesthetics
When building or styling frontend components for Toran Digital, adhere strictly to the "Light Mode Editorial" aesthetic:
- **Tone**: High-contrast, premium, brutalist-inspired editorial.
- **Colors**: Stark white (`#FFFFFF`) backgrounds, deep charcoal/black text (`#000000`, `#09090b`), with vibrant accent colors (Electric Orange-Red `#ff3300` and Electric Blue `#0044FF`). Avoid generic corporate blues.
- **Typography**: `Syne` for bold, geometric headings. `Manrope` for clean, legible body text. Use massive, tightly-kerned typography for impact.
- **Layout & CSS**: Favor solid borders (no border radius), harsh offset shadows (e.g., `8px 8px 0px var(--dark-900)`), and asymmetrical layouts over generic soft-shadow bento boxes.
- **Texture**: Use subtle SVG noise filters for background textures where appropriate to add depth.
